## H. Board and executive tests

1. **Problem:** Is the outcome and baseline clear?
2. **Alternatives:** Were credible preservation, reuse, non-AI and deferral options considered?
3. **Value:** Where will value be realised after full lifecycle cost and dependencies?
4. **Execution:** Can the organisation deliver and sustain the decision?
5. **Accountability:** Who owns the outcome and what are the authorised boundaries?
6. **Continuation:** What evidence will lead us to continue, change or stop?
