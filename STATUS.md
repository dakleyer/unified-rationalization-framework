# Version and institutional status

## Release

- **Version:** G0.11
- **Date:** 22 November 2026
- **State:** public discussion draft
- **Origin:** Switzerland
- **Organisation:** The Integral Management Society (IMSV)
- **Initiative:** Rationalization.AI / Unified Rationalization Framework

## Readiness

G0.11 is a documented procedural method suitable for expert review, catalogue assessment, comparison with related methods, and controlled practical evaluation. It is not represented as a finished international standard or as a statistically validated product.

## Institutional boundaries

This repository:

- is being prepared as supporting material for an OECD.AI Catalogue of Tools & Metrics submission;
- does not claim OECD or GPAI approval, adoption, endorsement, membership, or a speaking mandate;
- does not claim that related standards are insufficient or displaced;
- does not demonstrate conformity with the EU AI Act, ISO standards, NIST guidance, or sector law;
- does not publish private evidence held by IMSV; and
- keeps Structural Awareness and other related research programmes institutionally and technically distinct.
